## Scale

Aerial views explain geography, but detail photographs explain how a place feels. The publication therefore alternates between large establishing images and smaller observations.

## Sequence

A useful photo edit creates cause and effect: weather leads to shelter; shelter shapes settlement; settlement determines how people reach the water.
