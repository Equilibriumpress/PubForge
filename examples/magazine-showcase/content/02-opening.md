# At the edge of weather

The Atlantic does not arrive as scenery. It arrives as pressure, salt and changing light.

## A coast drawn by wind

On these islands the horizon is rarely still. Roads turn inland, walls lean into prevailing weather, and every harbour carries evidence of the last storm.
