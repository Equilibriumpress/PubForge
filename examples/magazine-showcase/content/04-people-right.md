# Marta, harbour master

> “People imagine the ocean as empty space. For us it is a timetable, a road and sometimes a wall.”

Her working day begins before the first ferry. Weather reports arrive on a screen, but the harbour still gets read through older signs: the sound against the breakwater, the direction of birds, the colour underneath the clouds.

:::pullquote
You learn to make decisions before certainty arrives.
