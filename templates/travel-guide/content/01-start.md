# Lisbon at a glance

<p class="travel-lead">Lisbon rewards a loose plan: one strong walking route, time for detours, and a willingness to let the hills decide the pace.</p>

<div class="fact-grid">
<div><span>BEST FOR</span><strong>2–4 days</strong></div>
<div><span>PACE</span><strong>Slow mornings</strong></div>
<div><span>TRANSIT</span><strong>Walk + tram</strong></div>
<div><span>DON'T MISS</span><strong>Golden hour</strong></div>
</div>

## How to orient yourself

{{image:route-map}}

<div class="travel-tip">
<strong>Start downhill, finish uphill.</strong>
Begin in Baixa while the streets are quiet, cross into Alfama before lunch, then climb toward Graça when the light becomes warmer.
</div>
